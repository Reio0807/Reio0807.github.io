Eloundou et al. provide the task-level exposure framework that anchors my Effective Thesis project.
They classify O\*NET work activities according to whether an LLM, alone or with complementary
software, could reduce completion time by at least 50%, then aggregate those ratings to occupations
and the workforce. The distinction I keep returning to is that *exposure is technological
potential, not observed automation or displacement*. My project starts from that gap and asks how
the paper's theoretical exposure measures compare with task-level delegation observed in
Anthropic's Economic Index.
